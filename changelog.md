Minor update to bring the mod into 1.21.5

## Changes
- The offhand damage bonus is now dependent on item having a default `combat-plus:melee_weapon` component.
- Parry and Lunge can obtain their damage from `combat-plus:melee_weapon` components if applicable. 
- Riposte and Deflection can no longer be put on items without Parry.
- Thrusting and Forceful can no longer be put on items without Lunge.
- Parry now grants a Blocks Attacks component to the item.

## Removals
- The following enchantment effects have been moved to Combat+ Core.
  - Set Cooldown
  - Max Use Time
  - Modify Cooldown
